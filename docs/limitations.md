# Assumptions and limitations

- The pivot is fixed; translation and contact mechanics are not integrated.
- Slipping, impacts, aerodynamic lift, and nonlinear pivot friction are absent.
- Damping is diagonal and linear in body-frame angular velocity.
- A general principal-axis inertia is supported, but the configured reference
  axis is not necessarily a geometric symmetry axis.
- FTLE is a finite-time numerical diagnostic and depends on its metric,
  perturbation, window, and persistence settings.
- Discovered formulas are empirical or semi-analytical and valid only over
  their reported parameter ranges.
