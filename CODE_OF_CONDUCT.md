# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone.
Be constructive, assume good faith, respect differing experience levels, and
focus technical discussion on evidence and reproducible behavior.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's
private information. Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
conduct is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical.
